# WILMS Approver Manual

**Version:** 1.7.3  
**Role:** Approver  
**Classification:** Confidential

---

## 1. Overview

Approvers review borrower registrations and loan applications, assess risk flags, and make approve/reject decisions. You operate through the Approver Portal.

---

## 2. Getting started

Login with approver credentials. Dashboard shows pending review counts and recent decisions.

---

## 3. Reviewing registrations

Applications → Pending Review. Open registration to view:
- Personal details
- Captured documents (side-by-side comparison)
- GPS location
- Registration officer notes

Approve to proceed to group/collector assignment. Reject with documented reason.

---

## 4. Assigning group and collector

On approval, assign borrower to a group and collector. Required before loan application.

---

## 5. Reviewing loan applications

Loans → Pending Approval. Review:
- Borrower history and profile
- Requested amount and purpose
- Group assignment
- Risk flags if any

Approve or reject with reason. Approved loans proceed to admin fee collection and disbursement.

---

## 6. Risk flags

Risk Flags → Review flagged borrowers or loans. Assess indicators and document decision. Can approve with flag or reject.

---

## 7. Overpayment reviews

Reconciliation → Overpayment Queue. Review collector submissions with cash variances. Approve adjustment or request correction.

---

## 8. What you cannot do

- Disburse funds directly
- Modify pool capital
- Manage users or settings
- Record field collections

---

## 9. Maker-checker principles

- Do not approve registrations you submitted
- Document rejection reasons clearly
- Review documents carefully before approval
- Escalate suspicious cases to admin

---

## 10. Best practices

- Process pending queue daily
- Compare ID photo with captured photo
- Verify group assignment matches geography
- Use risk flags consistently

---
