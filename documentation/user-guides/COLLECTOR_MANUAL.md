# WILMS Collector Manual

**Version:** 1.7.3  
**Role:** Collector  
**Classification:** Confidential

---

## 1. Overview

Collectors record weekly payments, admin fees, and field expenses for assigned borrower groups. You operate primarily through the mobile-optimised field shell with offline support.

---

## 2. Getting started

Login on your mobile device or tablet. The field shell displays with bottom navigation: Home, Groups, Payments, Reconcile, Profile.

---

## 3. Field dashboard

Home shows:
- Today's collection targets
- Assigned groups summary
- Offline status indicator
- Pending sync count

---

## 4. Recording a collection

### Online

1. Payments → Select borrower or group
2. Enter full weekly amount (partial payments not accepted)
3. GPS captured automatically
4. Confirm and submit
5. Receipt displayed

### Offline

1. Same steps as online
2. Payment queued locally (offline banner shows count)
3. Auto-syncs when connection restored
4. Verify sync success in Payments history

---

## 5. Admin fee collection

For approved loans awaiting disbursement, record admin fee from borrower profile or payment flow. Admin fee must be collected before disbursement.

---

## 6. Field expenses

Expenses → New Expense. Enter amount, category, description, receipt photo. Submit for HQ review. You cannot approve your own expenses.

---

## 7. Daily reconciliation

Reconcile → Enter physical cash count. System compares against recorded collections. Submit for HQ review. Resolve variances promptly.

---

## 8. Group collection sheet

Groups → Select group → Collection Sheet. View all members with payment status for the current week. Print or share for field reference.

---

## 9. Same-day edits

You can edit today's collections until day-end. After midnight, payments are immutable. Contact admin for corrections requiring reversal.

---

## 10. Offline guidelines

- Queue builds in local storage — do not clear browser data while offline items pending
- Sync requires stable connection
- Failed syncs show error — retry or contact admin
- GPS metadata preserved in offline queue

---

## 11. What you cannot do

- Approve loans or registrations
- Access admin settings or reports
- Modify pool capital
- View other collectors' assignments

---

## 12. Best practices

- Reconcile daily before leaving field
- Record payments at point of collection
- Keep device charged for GPS accuracy
- Sync offline queue before end of day

---

*CONFIDENTIAL — For authorized WILMS personnel, executive review, procurement, and official record keeping only.*
