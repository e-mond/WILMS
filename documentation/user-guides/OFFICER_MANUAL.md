# WILMS Registration Officer Manual

**Version:** 1.7.3  
**Role:** Registration Officer  
**Classification:** Confidential

---

## 1. Overview

Registration Officers register new borrowers, capture documents and GPS locations, and manage pending registrations until submission for approval.

---

## 2. Getting started

Login with your officer credentials. You access the Registration Portal with borrower-focused navigation.

---

## 3. Registering a borrower

### Step 1: Personal details

Applications → New Registration. Enter full name, date of birth, phone, address, district, community. All required fields must be completed.

### Step 2: Document capture

Upload identity document (national ID, voter ID, or passport). Capture photo. Select signature or thumbprint capture mode.

### Step 3: GPS verification

System captures GPS coordinates at registration location. Ensure device location services are enabled.

### Step 4: Save

Save as pending registration. You can edit pending registrations before submission.

---

## 4. Managing pending registrations

My Registrations shows your pending and submitted registrations with KPI summary. Edit pending items. Submit for approver review when complete.

---

## 5. Editing borrowers

You can edit borrower details for pending registrations. Approved borrowers require admin action for changes.

---

## 6. Printing agreements

From borrower profile, use Print Agreement for A4 formatted loan agreement document.

---

## 7. Search

Global search (navbar) finds borrowers by name, phone, or ID. Results are role-scoped to your accessible records.

---

## 8. What you cannot do

- Approve or reject loans
- Record collections
- Manage loan pools or users
- Access executive intelligence or audit log

---

## 9. Best practices

- Verify identity documents before submission
- Capture clear photos in good lighting
- Confirm GPS accuracy in rural areas
- Complete all fields before submitting for approval
