# Community and Suburb Guide

**Product version:** 1.8.0  
**Language:** British English

Communities are localities, suburbs, or neighbourhoods. They may share a name with an electoral area.

Rules:

- Officers cannot create communities during registration.
- Suggestions require Super Admin review (`PENDING` / `APPROVED` / `REJECTED`).
- Aliases store spelling variants (`Bakaeyile` / `Bakaekyir`).
- Unique `(district_id, name)` is retained from v1. Unique `(electoral_area_id, name)` applies when the electoral area is known.

Unsourced names such as European Town and Old Sekondi are **not** seeded.
